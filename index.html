Data Notes

Source
The supplied PRUmed lifelong care plan premium table.
Edition and effective date
Hong Kong Edition; valid from October 2025.
Currency and frequency
HKD, annual premiums.
Insurance Authority levy
The listed premiums exclude the levy collected by the Insurance Authority.
Rate changes
Premium rates are not guaranteed and may change. Actual renewal premiums depend on the table in force at renewal.
Reference only
Displayed figures are for reference and do not confirm that an application will be accepted. Figures at ages 76–100 are marked for renewal only in the source table.
Scope
This page reproduces premium figures only. It does not add or describe benefits or policy terms.
